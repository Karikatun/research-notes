# Anonymous material: Taste, Motion, and Impeccable skills

## Material claims

- The three skills provide composition, motion, and interface-critique rules.
- Using them together allegedly approaches professional design quality.

## Verification

The skill contents confirm their specialization, but overall quality gain and compatibility with an existing system remain unmeasured.

## Outcome

Do not install the bundle. A single non-mutating pilot of one skill against the existing process is acceptable.
