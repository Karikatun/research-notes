# Taste Skill

## Verdict

Not adopted as a global skill; acceptable as an exploration tool.

## Potential value

Helps the agent choose density, variance, and composition and avoid typical
AI slop before implementation.

## Demonstrated value and limitations

It has not been piloted in projects. Experimental rules may conflict with an
established design system and make a neutral template too opinionated.

## Decision

Throwaway prototypes only, followed by manual acceptance.
